QR Region Scanner

Read QR codes on any webpage.

Shortcut

No shortcut set. Another extension may already use Alt+Shift+Q, so pick a different one.

How to use

  1. On any webpage, press the shortcut, or click this extension’s icon and choose Scan this page.
  2. Drag a box around the QR code.
  3. Click Open for a link, or select the text to copy it.

Tip: pin the extension so it’s one click away. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to QR Region Scanner.
