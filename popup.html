QR Region Scanner

Read QR codes on any webpage.

Keyboard shortcut Scans part of the page

Another extension may already use Alt+Shift+Q, so pick a different one.

How it works
  1. On any webpage, click this extension’s icon and choose Scan this page.
  2. Not found, or several codes on screen? Choose Scan part of page (or press the shortcut) and drag a box around one.
  3. Click Open for a link, or select the text to copy it.

Keep it one click away: browsers hide new extensions, so click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar, then the pin (Chrome) or eye icon (Edge) next to QR Region Scanner.

Works offline. Nothing leaves your browser.
